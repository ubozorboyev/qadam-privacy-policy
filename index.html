Privacy Policy — Qadam

Last updated: 12.10.2026

Qadam ("the App") is an English learning app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal information. There is no sign-up or account, and the App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files or payment details.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the App saves the following on your device only:

  • Your settings: selected level, translation language and daily goal
  • Your learning progress: completed lessons and results
  • Your word review schedule (which words to review and when)

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

3. Sharing

  • We do not share or sell any data.
  • The App shows no ads and contains no advertising SDKs.
  • The App does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

4. Internet Use

If the App downloads learning content (for example lesson updates or audio), those requests do not include any personal information.

5. Google Play

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect technical information such as crash reports and install statistics under Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the App's data practices change (for example, if crash reporting or analytics are added), this Privacy Policy will be updated before that version of the App is released, and the date above will change.
