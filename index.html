Privacy Policy — Qadam

Last updated: 12.10.2026 (v2 — analytics added)

Qadam ("the App") is an English learning app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information We Collect

There is no sign-up or account. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or payment details.

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used so we can improve lessons. It automatically collects:

  • App interactions: which screens are opened, sessions, first open and app updates
  • Device or other identifiers: a random app-instance ID and the Android Advertising ID
  • Approximate location: country/region derived from a masked IP address (not precise location)
  • Basic device information: device model, operating system version, app version and language

This data is not linked to your name or any account, and all of it is encrypted in transit (TLS).

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Your settings (level, translation language, daily goal), learning progress and word review schedule are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us or anyone else and are deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

3. How We Use Information

Analytics data is used only to measure and improve the App (for example, which lessons people finish). We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell any data.

4. Third Parties

  • Analytics data is processed by Google as our service provider under Google's Privacy Policy and the Firebase data processing terms.
  • The App shows no ads and contains no advertising SDKs.
  • We do not share data with any other party.

5. Your Choices

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Uninstalling the App stops all collection and removes the app-instance ID from your device.
  • Event-level Google Analytics data is deleted automatically after the retention period set for the App (2 months by default).
  • For any privacy request, contact us at the email below.

6. Google Play

Google Play may collect technical information such as crash reports and install statistics under Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If the App's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that version of the App is released, and the date above will change.
